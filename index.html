C++ Projects

Ecosystem Simulation

Overview: An advanced life simulation in C++ where you observe a self-sustaining world on a massive 200×70 map — animal and plant populations fight for survival, reproduce, and evolve.

Highlights:
  • Real Genetics – Each animal inherits 20+ unique traits (speed, toxin resistance, hunger tolerance, herd instinct) with random mutations
  • Dynamic Biomes – Five environments (meadow, forest, desert, swamp, mountains) affect plant growth and survival rates
  • Real-Time Evolution – Watch new species emerge and track statistics in the info panel
  • Full Autonomy – Plants spread, animals hunt, seek mates, defend territory — all without player input

Tech Stack: C++20
